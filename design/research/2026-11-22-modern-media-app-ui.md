# Quiet chrome lets cinematic artwork lead

In late 2026, a modern dark media app pairs quiet, near-neutral chrome with artwork that supplies the color and depth. Translucency is used only on the floating control layer, always with a legibility floor. Feedback comes from transform and spring motion rather than ink ripples. Surfaces are lightness-stepped and edged with hairlines instead of tinted tonal fills. Every major design language converged on parts of this in 2025-2026. Apple's Liquid Glass retreated in iOS 27 toward darker rims and a user tint slider. Google's Material 3 Expressive replaced ripples with shape morphing and springs. Fluent 2 kept frosted material for transient overlays only. The Linear/shadcn school drifted toward calmer, warmer neutrals with fewer lines. The streamers converged on floating, translucent playback controls, taller theatrical posters and autoplaying heroes, while the Plex backlash showed that self-hosted users punish lost remote-control efficiency far more than they reward visual polish. For Mydia's Frosted mode, the research **confirms most of the proposed mockup** (no ripple, scale-on-press, hairline borders, anchored popovers, offset focus ring with scale). It **refines** radii, toasts, dialogs and the TV focus recipe, and it **contradicts** one point: segmented controls cannot replace dropdowns wherever the option list is long or dynamic. The way to deliver it is to deepen the existing `ThemeData` and wrap high-visibility widgets in house components, not to adopt a shadcn-style kit. None of those kits supports D-pad navigation, and their aesthetic is SaaS dashboard rather than cinema.

## Four design languages converged on "glass for chrome, calm for content"

**Apple's Liquid Glass shipped loudly and was walked back toward legibility.** Apple defines it as "a distinct functional layer for controls and navigation elements" floating over edge-to-edge content, with rounder controls "concentric to their containers", floating tab bars that minimize on scroll, and an instruction to "limit these effects to the most important functional elements" ([Apple, Adopting Liquid Glass](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/technologyoverviews/adopting-liquid-glass.md)). NN/g's October 2025 verdict was blunt: translucent controls lose contrast over busy backgrounds, and the result is "restless, needy, less predictable, less legible" ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/liquid-glass/)). iOS 27, released September 2026, kept the material but added **a darkened edge around glass elements, brighter specular rims, a uniform backdrop behind top bars when content scrolls under them, and a continuous "ultra clear to fully tinted" slider** ([MacRumors, June 2026](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/10/how-liquid-glass-is-changing-in-ios-27/)). Smaller details matter for any restyle: section headers moved from ALL CAPS to title case, list rows grew taller, and sheets gained larger radii and insets ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/technologyoverviews/adopting-liquid-glass.md)). Third parties took the idea without the refraction. Linear's iOS glass is blur, gradient and an SDF specular rim, with refraction omitted because it "can reduce readability in dense interfaces" ([Linear](https://linear.app/blog/linear-liquid-glass)). shadcn's "Luma" style is explicitly "macOS Tahoe minus the glass": rounded geometry, soft elevation, no translucency ([shadcn changelog](https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/changelog/2026-03-luma)).

**Material 3 Expressive abandoned ink in favor of shape and spring.** It adds a 35-shape library with shape morphing, a motion-physics system that replaces easing and duration, button groups whose members change width on press, a floating toolbar, and a shape-morphing loading indicator that replaces most indeterminate spinners ([Supercharge Design](https://supercharge.design/blog/material-3-expressive); [Android Developers Blog](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2025/08/introducing-material-3-expressive-for-wear-os.html)). The reception was lukewarm. 9to5Google called it "Material 3.5", criticized oversized buttons and dense, cluttered screens, and described most app updates as "component swaps rather than overhauls" ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2025/12/27/recap-material-3-expressive/)). By contrast, stock pre-Expressive M3 reads as about 2021-2023 Android: uniform pill buttons, tonal surface tint, ripples and circular spinners. That is the look Frosted mode carries today.

**Fluent 2 supplies the cleanest material taxonomy for a media app.** Solid is the opaque default. Mica is an opaque base subtly tinted by the wallpaper. Acrylic is frosted glass reserved for transient, light-dismiss surfaces such as menus and popovers. Smoke is a black scrim behind modals ([Fluent 2, Material](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/material)). Map that onto Mydia and you get an artwork-tinted opaque ground (the existing ambient tint), frost only on menus and transport chrome, and a dark scrim behind dialogs. Microsoft's 2026 work was consistency rather than a new aesthetic.

**The web-minimalist school got calmer and warmer, not flatter.** Linear's March 2026 refresh follows "Structure should be felt not seen". It moved its default grays from cool blue-leaning to warmer, less saturated tones, dimmed the sidebar so content leads, reduced and softened separators, and shrank icons ([Linear](https://linear.app/now/behind-the-latest-design-refresh)). Earlier, Linear rebuilt its themes in LCH from three inputs (base, accent, contrast) and deliberately limited colored chrome for "a more neutral, timeless appearance" ([Linear, 2024](https://linear.app/now/how-we-redesigned-the-linear-ui)). shadcn now ships named styles that change geometry and density, not just color ([shadcn Sera](https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/changelog/2026-04-sera)). From this school a cinematic app should take the chrome vocabulary: quiet, dim and hairline, with small icons. Its density and small type are not suited to 10-foot TV viewing.

## Streamers float their controls; self-hosters punish extra clicks

Every major streamer redesigned between 2024 and 2026, and they converged. Apple's tvOS 26 TV app adopted "vertical poster art" described as more theatrical, showed more titles on screen, and made playback controls that are transparent and "float above your video without obstructing what's happening underneath" ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2025/07/16/apples-tv-app-gets-fresh-design-in-ios-26-and-tvos-26-heres-whats-new/)). On TV, Apple applied Liquid Glass **mainly to playback controls, Control Center and icons, far less than on iPhone** ([AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/25/06/18/tvos-26-hands-on-sleek-liquid-glass-redesign-new-control-center-and-more)). Netflix's May 2025 TV overhaul, its first in about 12 years, put persistent shortcuts across the top and favored larger tiles and video previews ([Netflix Tudum](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/netflix-new-tv-layout)). Disney+ adopted "cinematic-style poster" art, state badges such as "Season Finale", autoplaying hero video and a hybrid top bar plus vertical rail ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2025/10/02/disney-reveals-app-redesign-coming-soon-to-ios-and-tvos/)). YouTube's December 2025 TV player puts the title top-left and **three control clusters under a full-width scrubber: metadata left, transport center, captions and settings right** ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2025/12/12/youtube-redesigned-video-player-for-tvs-20th-birthday/)). Prime Video uses yellow purely as a signal, a shopping bag marking paid titles ([iClarified](https://www.iclarified.com/94317/amazon-prime-video-rolls-out-redesigned-streaming-experience)), which is the model for how a gold accent should behave.

Plex is the cautionary tale for Mydia's audience. Its TV redesign replaced a pinned, user-ordered library sidebar with a top bar and overlay menus. Users reported some actions growing from about 2 clicks to about 6, along with poster-scroll lag and no opt-out ([PiunikaWeb, May 2026](https://piunikaweb.com/2026/05/04/plex-fire-tv-redesign-unusable-users-slam-navigation/)). Forum threads ran past 23 pages ([Plex forums](https://forums.plex.tv/t/new-ui-is-an-awful-experience/931048?page=23)), and Plex brought the left menu back in a preview build ([Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/plex-brings-back-left-side-menu-3700033)). Visual style drew little fire. Navigation cost and performance drew nearly all of it. A restyle of Mydia's controls is therefore low-risk only if it leaves click counts, focus order and scroll performance untouched. Coverage of Infuse, Emby, Kodi skins and Spotify was thin in this research, so no sourced claim about "the best-looking self-hosted client" is possible.

**TV focus has two codified systems.** Android TV specifies scale steps of **1.025, 1.05 and 1.1x depending on element size**, a glow of 2-32dp whose color may follow the image or brand, and border/outline as an independent indication ([Android Developers, Focus system](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/tv/guides/styles/focus-system)). Apple's tvOS defaults to parallax lift on layered images, with halo and highlight as alternatives ([Apple HIG, Focus and selection](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/focus-and-selection)).

## Component craft now runs on transforms, steps and hairlines

The craft consensus, drawn mostly from Emil Kowalski (Sonner, Vaul) and Vercel's interface guidelines, is specific. Pressed controls **scale to about 0.97**, and nothing ever animates from `scale(0)`: entrances start at 0.9 or higher ([Kowalski, 7 tips](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/7-practical-animation-tips)). Popovers and menus scale in from their trigger rather than from center, and a 180 ms select animation beats a 400 ms one. Tooltips run at 125 ms ease-out, and once one is open its neighbors open instantly ([Kowalski](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/7-practical-animation-tips); [Vercel](https://vercel.com/design/guidelines)). Sheets are the deliberate exception: Vaul uses `cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)` at **500 ms** to match iOS, with rubber-band resistance and flick-to-dismiss ([Kowalski, drawer](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-a-drawer-component)). Toasts stack with each one offset by index × gap and shrunk by 0.05 per index, and they use a slightly slower `ease` on purpose ([Kowalski, toast](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-a-toast-component)). Toasts should be announced politely to screen readers. Spinners and skeletons wait 150-300 ms before appearing, then stay at least 300-500 ms so they do not flicker. Destructive actions need either confirmation or an Undo with a safe window. Hit targets must be at least 24 px on desktop and 44 px on mobile, even when the visual element is smaller ([Vercel](https://vercel.com/design/guidelines)). No primary source gave canonical numbers for button height, switch size, radius or segmented-control styling. Those are house style, and any claim otherwise is invented.

Dark-mode surfaces follow a stepped scale. Radix's 12-step model is the clearest. Step 1 is the app background and step 2 holds cards and sidebars. Steps 3-5 are component rest, hover and pressed. Step 6 is non-interactive borders, steps 7-8 interactive borders and focus rings, steps 9-10 solid fills, and 11-12 text. **Amber and yellow solids take dark text, not white** ([Radix Colors](https://www.radix-ui.com/colors/docs/palette-composition/understanding-the-scale)). Elevation comes from stepping lightness up per layer, not from shadows. Secondary sources converge on **a ~8% white hairline**, reused as an inset top rim, so dark cards read as physical objects ([Muzli](https://muz.li/blog/dark-mode-design-systems-a-complete-guide-to-patterns-tokens-and-hierarchy/); [DEV](https://dev.to/raxxostudios/the-8-css-properties-that-make-dark-uis-feel-premium-1kh7)). Where shadows are used, Vercel asks for at least two layers (ambient plus direct), semi-transparent borders, and concentric nested radii ([Vercel](https://vercel.com/design/guidelines)). For contrast, the community has moved to APCA because WCAG 2 overstates contrast near black. The targets are **Lc 75 minimum (Lc 90 preferred) for body text, Lc 60 for other readable text, Lc 45 for headlines and fine icons, Lc 30 for placeholder and disabled text, and Lc 15 for dividers and thick focus outlines** ([APCA in a Nutshell](https://git.apcacontrast.com/documentation/APCA_in_a_Nutshell)). Palettes are generated in OKLCH or LCH, and Tailwind v4 made OKLCH its default ([Tailwind](https://tailwindcss.com/blog/tailwindcss-v4)).

Typography is settled rather than trendy. Inter remains the standard UI face, with Inter Display for headings, as Linear uses ([Linear](https://linear.app/now/how-we-redesigned-the-linear-ui)). Inter exposes `tnum`, `zero`, `case` and `ss02` ([rsms.me/inter](https://rsms.me/inter/)), and tabular numerals are expected wherever digits update or align ([Vercel](https://vercel.com/design/guidelines)). For a player that means timecodes, durations, file sizes and bitrates. APCA's size and weight pairs set a floor: body text at Lc 75 needs about 16 px/500 or 18 px/400.

Motion should stay under about 300 ms, ease out on enter and exit, animate only transform and opacity, and stay interruptible. Frequent or keyboard-initiated actions should not animate at all ([Kowalski, Great animations](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/great-animations)). That last rule matters for D-pad traversal, which is keyboard-initiated hundreds of times per session. NN/g's critique of iOS 26 motion that "grabs attention without helping" is the counterweight to M3E's springiness ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/liquid-glass/)). On what looks dated, the sourced items are giant icons and button gradients ([daisyUI trends](https://trends.daisyui.com/ui-design-trends-2026/)), colorful chrome ([Linear](https://linear.app/now/how-we-redesigned-the-linear-ui)) and ink ripples, which Google itself left behind. Trend writers disagree about glassmorphism: some call it a staple and others say it is fading ([Tubik](https://blog.tubikstudio.com/ui-design-trends-2026)). The defensible position is Apple's own: frost on chrome, never on content.

## Flutter delivers this through ThemeData and house wrappers, not a kit

Mydia is already on the post-decoupling stack. The in-framework Material and Cupertino libraries were frozen at Flutter 3.44, shipped as `material_ui`/`cupertino_ui` 1.0 with 3.47, and were formally deprecated in-framework in 3.50 ([Flutter breaking-change doc](https://docs.flutter.dev/release/breaking-changes/material-ui-and-cupertino-ui)). The packages now ship weekly, and Google says "work is already underway on official implementations of Liquid Glass and Material 3 Expressive" there ([Flutter blog](https://flutter.dev/blog/decoupling-material-cupertino)). M3E foundations (shapes, motion springs) merged in flutter/packages in October-November 2026, and component PRs are open ([flutter#168813](https://github.com/flutter/flutter/issues/168813)). **The practical consequence is visual drift.** `material_ui` went from 1.0 to 1.11.1 in about three months, so a caret range on it can change how stock widgets look without any Flutter upgrade. Pinning it, and wrapping the widgets that matter, insulates Frosted mode from that drift.

The kits do not fit. shadcn_ui (980 likes, 0.61.2) and forui (449 likes, 0.28.0, which requires exactly Flutter 3.50 and Dart 3.14) are active and coexist with `material_ui`. Both are pre-1.0 with breaking minors, ship their own icons and theme objects, look like SaaS dashboards, and **none markets D-pad or TV support** ([pub.dev forui](https://pub.dev/packages/forui); [pub.dev shadcn_ui](https://pub.dev/packages/shadcn_ui)). shadcn_flutter is Material-free but still at 0.0.55. fluent_ui, macos_ui and yaru imitate a single platform, and moon_design is archived. The one ecosystem piece that matches a bespoke look is headless behavior: naked_ui, "behavior-first UI components ... that separate state from presentation", optionally with Mix 2.x for styling ([pub.dev naked_ui](https://pub.dev/packages/naked_ui)). Wonderous remains the canonical proof that a Flutter app can ignore stock design systems and stay accessible ([Flutter blog](https://flutter.dev/blog/wonderous-explore-the-world-with-flutter)).

Frost has a measurable cost on the platforms where Frosted mode is the default. Impeller composed backdrop blurs regressed from about 7-8 ms to over 20 ms between 3.41.2 and 3.47.x on Android Vulkan/GLES and iOS Metal ([flutter#194832](https://github.com/flutter/flutter/issues/194832)), and whether 3.50 fixes it is unconfirmed. Putting a color filter outside a blur in `ImageFilter.compose` costs more GPU time than putting it inside, and this was still true on master on 2026-11-16 ([flutter#196728](https://github.com/flutter/flutter/issues/196728)). liquid_glass_renderer's docs are frank: every `BackdropFilter` layer "copies the whole render pass behind it", and its blur-only FakeGlass "is not a cheaper mode" ([pub.dev](https://pub.dev/packages/liquid_glass_renderer)). On TV, Flutter's default directional traversal picks the geometrically nearest widget. Packages such as `dpad` add focus regions with memory on top of Shortcuts, Actions and FocusTraversalPolicy ([dpad](https://pub.dev/documentation/dpad/latest/)), and `tv_textfield` fixes D-pad focus getting trapped in text fields ([pub.dev](https://pub.dev/packages/tv_textfield)). Any widget replaced by a custom one can silently lose Material's Semantics roles and focus handling. That is the main hazard of a restyle.

## Recommendations for Frosted mode: what the research says about the mockup

The checked-in theme already has the right structure for this work. `AppTheme.build(palette, liquid:)` switches button shape and `NoSplash` only for Liquid Glass, and a `withTvFocus` helper adds a 3 px accent border on directional-input devices. `DepthTokens` already defines a no-blur tier for scrolling surfaces, and an ambient-tint pipeline exists. The table below gives the verdict on each mockup element. The paragraphs after it give the reasoning and the Flutter route.

| Mockup element | Verdict | What the research adds |
|---|---|---|
| No ripple; scale 0.97 + dim on press | **Confirmed** | Use about 100-150 ms ease-out on transform and opacity only, interruptible. Google itself left ink behind. |
| 10-12 px radii | **Refined** | There is no canonical number. Apply the concentric rule: controls ~10-12, cards and menus ~12-14, sheets and dialogs ~16-20, posters keep their own token. |
| Hairline borders instead of tonal fills | **Confirmed, with a caveat** | Hairlines need lightness-stepped surfaces underneath. Use fewer lines overall, an ~8% white edge plus an optional inset top rim, and an Lc ≥ 15 floor. |
| 40×24 switch | **Unsourced (house style)** | Keep a hit area of ≥ 44 px on touch. On TV the switch needs its own focus ring, not just a thumb color. |
| Segmented controls instead of radios/dropdowns | **Partly contradicted** | Good for 2-4 fixed, short options. Keep anchored menus or lists for long or dynamic sets (subtitle and audio tracks, libraries, languages). |
| Popover menus | **Confirmed** | Scale from 0.95 at the trigger origin over 150-200 ms. Add an ellipsis on items that open a follow-up step. |
| Pill toast | **Confirmed, refined** | Use a Sonner-style stack (0.05 scale per index, slightly slower `ease`), a polite live region, and never cover the focused element. |
| Centered dialogs, stacked full-width buttons | **Refined** | Put a Smoke-style dark scrim behind them. Prefer Undo over confirm for reversible actions. The stacked layout is judgment, not a sourced convention. |
| Offset gold focus ring + slight scale on TV | **Confirmed, refined** | Use size-dependent scale (1.1 posters, 1.05 tiles, 1.025 wide rows and buttons). Keep the ring independent of glow. Don't animate traversal itself beyond a quick settle. |

**Press feedback and buttons.** Set `splashFactory: NoSplash.splashFactory` for Frosted mode too, not only Liquid Glass. Set `overlayColor` to a low-alpha white for hover and a slight dim on press. Add the 0.97 scale in a house `PressScale` wrapper, because `ButtonStyle` cannot express transforms. Keep the white filled primary: it is neutral, and gold stays a signal. Where gold is used as a fill (selected chips, progress pills), put dark text on it, per Radix's amber rule. Reserve gold for primary play/resume when it is not white, for progress, selection and focus, following Prime Video's use of yellow as a signal rather than decoration. Avoid gradients on buttons.

**Surfaces, borders and color.** The ground (#0B0B0C) and surfaces (#111113 to #26272B) already form a stepped scale. The gap from ground to the first surface is small, though, so verify each step is visible in OKLCH, roughly +3-5% lightness per layer. Express the palette as Radix-style roles: steps for hover and pressed, a non-interactive border at about 8% white instead of the fixed #2E2E33, interactive borders a step stronger, and focus on the gold step. Following Linear, consider nudging the faint blue lean of #111113-family surfaces toward neutral or slightly warm. That sits better next to gold and is the 2026 direction, though Linear warns that too warm "risks looking muddy". Drop full-width dividers in lists in favor of spacing. Check secondary text and dimmed gold with APCA (Lc 60 for labels, 75 for body), not WCAG ratios.

**Radii.** Collapse the current 16/18/20/22/24 spread toward the concentric rule: inner radius equals outer radius minus padding. Choose controls and inputs at 10-12 px, menus and cards at 12-14 px, and sheets and dialogs at 16-20 px. This is the same rule Apple formalized, and it keeps Frosted visually related to Liquid Glass without copying its capsules.

**Selection controls.** Use segmented controls (M3E's connected button group is the nearest Google analogue) for short fixed choices such as sort order, view mode, or quality presets with 2-4 options. On TV, left and right D-pad presses map naturally onto segments. Keep anchored popover menus or full-height list panels for anything long or data-driven. On TV, a side panel listing tracks is more efficient than a dropdown, and the Plex episode shows how heavily this audience weighs click count.

**Menus, toasts, dialogs and sheets.** Route `PopupMenuButton`, `DropdownButton`, `AlertDialog`, `showModalBottomSheet` and `SnackBar` through house APIs (`showAppMenu`, `showAppDialog`, `showAppSheet`, `showAppToast`). Platform presentation then becomes a single switch: frosted panel on desktop, opaque panel on TV, glass on Apple. Animate menus from their anchor, run sheets on `Cubic(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)` at about 500 ms, and give toasts a stack with polite Semantics live regions. Apply Fluent's taxonomy here. Frost (blur) belongs only on these transient surfaces and on transport chrome, never on lists, rails or cards. On Android TV and web, prefer opaque or pre-blurred backdrops over live `BackdropFilter`, and when blur is used, put color filters inside the blur composition.

**TV focus.** Keep the mockup's offset gold ring and treat it as the primary cue. The dark gap left by the offset keeps it readable over gold or bright artwork, which a glow alone cannot do. Scale focused items by size class as Android TV specifies. Make glow optional and tint it from artwork using the existing ambient-tint extraction. Disable scale and parallax under `MediaQuery.disableAnimations`. Because traversal is keyboard-initiated and constant, the focus move should feel near-instant. Animate only a short settle of scale and ring, never a slow slide. Make sure press-scale (0.97) composes with focus-scale rather than fighting it: scale the focused item, then apply 0.97 of that on press. Every replacement widget must be built on `FocusableActionDetector`, or headless naked_ui-style primitives, and tested with a D-pad and a screen reader before it replaces a Material widget.

**Typography and motion details.** Enable `FontFeature.tabularFigures()` on every timecode, duration, size and bitrate label. Switch any ALL-CAPS section headers to title or sentence case. Keep Outfit for display, with Inter Display as the fallback if Outfit ever reads too soft. Hold UI motion to 120-200 ms `Curves.easeOut` for menus and press feedback. Replace indeterminate circular spinners with a delayed (150-300 ms) skeleton or a calmer indicator. The M3E springs landing in `material_ui` can be adopted later, through the house wrappers, once they ship in a pinned release.

**Migration order.** Start by pinning `material_ui` exactly. Next, push the theme further: Frosted `NoSplash`, overlay colors, hairline `side`s, radius tokens, a `ThemeExtension` for the step and border roles, and switch, checkbox and radio themes that match the mockup. Together these restyle all ~90 Material-using files at once. Then build wrappers for the five high-visibility families (buttons with press-scale, switches, menus, dialogs, toasts and sheets) and migrate call sites screen by screen. Leave a full kit migration off the table.

## Conclusion

The real shift in 2026 is not glass versus flat. It is a split between **chrome that recedes** and **content that carries color and depth**. Apple, Google, Microsoft and Linear arrived there from different directions, and the streamers' floating, translucent OSDs are the same idea applied to video. For Mydia this means Frosted mode does not need to imitate Liquid Glass to look current. A disciplined neutral system (stepped surfaces, hairlines, concentric radii, gold as signal, transform-based feedback) is the non-Apple expression of the same principles, and it is cheaper on the Android TV hardware where Frosted is the default.

The binding constraints are not aesthetic. Plex shows this audience will forgive a dated look but not two extra clicks. Flutter's weekly `material_ui` releases mean Google's next visual changes will arrive whether Mydia wants them or not. Owning the high-visibility widgets behind house wrappers solves both problems at once: it protects D-pad behavior and Semantics, freezes the look against upstream drift, and leaves a single seam for adopting M3E springs or official Liquid Glass later on Mydia's own schedule. Some gaps remain unsourced: canonical control dimensions, backdrop-blur cost on real Android TV hardware, and how Infuse and Kodi skins look today. On-device measurement and a screenshot audit are the next evidence worth gathering.
