A security fix on top of `v0.19.0`, and nothing else. Upgrade if anyone other than you signs in to your Mydia, especially with a `readonly` or `guest` account.

## Security

- Subtitle changes now need write access. Before this, any signed-in account, including `readonly` and `guest`, could delete subtitle files anywhere in the library, change a track's timing for every viewer, and run subtitle searches against your provider quota. Accounts with category or rating limits could do the same to titles outside those limits. Delete, timing, auto-sync, rescan, upload, search and the season fetch now require an `admin` or `user` role and only act on files belonging to the title being viewed. View-only accounts see the subtitle list without the controls (GHSA-v7xr-mv9w-6q2h, #1513)

## Upgrade notes

- No migrations.
- In the player, a `readonly` or `guest` account can still nudge subtitle timing for its own playback, but saving the correction is refused, since a saved correction applies to everyone (#1513)
